PNG to SVG

From pixels to paths.

Trace your image into filled shapes and smooth curves, with a limited palette to keep the file manageable.

How it works & credits

Colours are reduced before region tracing. Small regions are filtered, curves are fitted and SVG markup is optimised. The download contains vector shapes, never an embedded copy of the source image.

Fewer colours and a lower tracing resolution usually produce smaller files. Fine textures may become flat shapes.
